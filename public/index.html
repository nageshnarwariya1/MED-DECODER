              YOUR DOCUMENTS

              A clear note about privacy

              Your original upload is processed in memory and is not saved on the website. A de-identified analysis result (test names, values, ranges and explanations) is stored for up to 30 days so the admin can monitor the service and review errors. The admin area is password-protected. Your report is also sent to Google Gemini for AI processing. Remove names, phone numbers, patient IDs and other personal details before uploading. Do not upload anyone else's report without their permission.
